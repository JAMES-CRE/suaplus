Request Access

Sua+ Hub accounts are provisioned by the KNUST E-Learning Centre. Self-registration is disabled.

Who needs an account

  • Facilitators (create sessions)
  • Programme Owners (nominate trainers)
  • College Focal Persons (review credentials)
  • Super Admins (manage the system)

Who doesn't

Participants - your passport arrives by email after each workshop. No account is needed to view your stamps, competencies, and trainer progress.

To request access

Email elearning@knust.edu.gh with:

  • Your full name and staff number
  • The role you need (facilitator, programme owner, focal person)
  • Your college or unit
  • The programme you'll be working with, if applicable
