What each action does

  • Export - downloads a JSON copy of the person's data: their profile, consents, attendances, evidence, and credentials.
  • Anonymise - replaces the person's name, email, phone, and staff number with placeholder values. Their attendance and evidence rows stay intact so aggregate counts are preserved. This cannot be undone.
  • Correct - for corrections, use the Django admin to edit the person's record directly.
